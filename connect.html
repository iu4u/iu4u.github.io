Connect your TikTok account

iu4u Content Pipeline uses TikTok Login Kit to identify your account, then uploads your approved videos as drafts for review.

Authorise link (paste your app's client key into the URL after setup):
https://www.tiktok.com/v2/auth/authorize/?client_key=CLIENT_KEY&scope=user.info.basic,video.upload&response_type=code&redirect_uri=https://iu4u.github.io/callback
