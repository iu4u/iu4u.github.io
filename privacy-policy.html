Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you use iu4u tools and how we handle it. We operate from the United Kingdom.

1. What we collect

When you connect a TikTok account we receive the authorisation tokens, your account identifier and display name. When you buy from our store we receive order and delivery details via Shopify.

2. Why we use it

To post content you have asked us to post, to operate the store, and to keep the service secure.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Processors we rely on include TikTok (content posting), GitHub (hosting) and Shopify (store operations), each under their own terms.

4. Retention

Tokens are kept until you disconnect or they expire; order records are kept as long as tax law requires.

5. Your rights (UK GDPR)

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data via the contact address on our store.
